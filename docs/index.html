The Synthetic Panopticon

AI-Enabled Identity Fraud, PII Degradation, and Multi-Layered Identity Defense

Author: Dinesh Gowd Patil

Version: v1

Published: June 2026


Overview

The Synthetic Panopticon is a technical report and evidence package on AI-enabled identity fraud, PII degradation, synthetic identity risk, deepfake-enabled impersonation, and layered identity assurance. The companion SP-SEC-50 Evidence Corpus provides structured source and metric data for review, reuse, and extension.
